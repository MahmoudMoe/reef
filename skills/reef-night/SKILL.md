# Reef Night — delegate one night, approve once

**Before anything**: `.reef/run/gate.json` must say ok for the mode the owner will run (else `python3 scripts/reef-run.py probe` — a headless session the guard does not reach would be a way around every rule). Run one orchestrator at a time: no other `/reef-loop` or runner on this project tonight.

1. **The owner's profile** — `reef/owner.md`. Missing: `reef-run.py contract` (step 2) seeds it from the owner's personal profile, `~/.reef/owner.md` (who they are in every project), so a new project's first night starts from it. Missing both, or older than the newest queue footnote: dispatch `reef:mahmoud` (model `roles.proxy`) to draft or extend it into the scratchpad from the personal profile, the queue footnotes (verbatim decisions), the journal's owner decisions and `AGENTS.md`: principles, product taste, ethics, discipline, what they always keep for themselves. `cp` it to `reef/owner.md`.
2. **The contract** — dispatch `reef:mahmoud` to draft tonight's contract into the scratchpad from the tick and the queue (`$ARGUMENTS` narrows it): a `Rows: <ids>` line — by default every ready row of the current batch, planned and found (`python3 scripts/reef-queue.py ready --with-found`), one left out only with its reason (a credential it needs, an owner question open on it); at night the tick drives these rows and no others, the decisions it may take (plan approvals, design looks, product questions, holes after the hunter's rounds, found-row triage), and the hard limits it never crosses — at least: the release to `branches.release`, secrets and credentials, money and paid accounts, deleting data or branches, Reef's own guard and config, personal data leaving the system, web research that names anything from this repository or the owner, any row not listed. A preview look is delegated by default: `AGENTS.md` keeping "the look" for the owner does not hold it back at night, since the approved contract is the owner delegating it; only a footnote or a profile line keeping a named look (or every look at night) holds it back, and the contract says so. Then `python3 scripts/reef-run.py contract --from <that file>`: it installs `.reef/night/contract.md` and prints the night digest and the exact question.
3. **The one approval** — ONE AskUserQuestion, its first question EXACTLY `Approve night <YYYY-MM-DD> at <digest16>?` (from step 2), options `Approve` / `Edit` / `Cancel`, the `Approve` description: the rows, the decisions delegated, the hard limits, one line each. The hook records `.reef/approvals/night.json` only on Approve at the current digest; an edit to the contract or the profile afterwards approves nothing. On Edit: Mahmoud amends, step 2 again.
4. **Start the night** — on Approve, run `python3 scripts/reef-run.py --night --detach` yourself. It refuses here, before detaching, if anything is wrong; otherwise it returns at once with the runner's pid, and the runner works on after this session ends. Tell the owner: `python3 scripts/reef-run.py status` shows it from any session (`watch` follows it live in a terminal); the morning report is `.reef/night/report.md`. Then do no more loop work in this session: while the runner holds the lock it is the one orchestrator, and the Stop hook lets this session end.

**During the night** (the runner's prompt carries this): every owner decision inside the contract goes to `reef:mahmoud`, never to `questions.md`. A plan approval: Mahmoud writes its report, you `cp` it to `.reef/ci-local/report-mahmoud-<slug>.md`, then `python3 ${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/reef-approval.py --proxy --feature <slug> --report <that file>` — it writes the approval `by: mahmoud (proxy)` only while the night runs, for a row the contract names, at the plan's current digest, with the plan check green. Any other decision: `python3 scripts/reef-queue.py footnote "Mahmoud (proxy, night <date>): <decision> — <why>" --row <id>` — the label first, never passed off as the owner's words. A `WAIT` goes to `questions.md` and the other rows keep moving.

Mahmoud's `CHALLENGE` (a plan, once per row, recorded) and `RESEARCH` (one `Explore` agent) lines: exactly as `/reef-loop` § The proxy by day says — never a loop.

**The morning** — the runner writes `.reef/night/report.md`: Mahmoud's approvals (undo: delete the record), his other decisions (undo: edit the footnote), the merges (undo: `git revert -m 1 <sha>` on `branches.base`), what waits for the owner, sessions and cost, and the night in numbers beside the last one (rows merged, tasks finished, first-pass share, stalls, cost per merged row; `.reef/night/history.jsonl`).

End with: the approval's result, the runner's pid and the status command. `in flight: … / waiting on: … / next: <command>`.
