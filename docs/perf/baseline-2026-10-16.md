## Targets this baseline sets

- **Session start** (6.2): a Reef project session must inject under 8,000 tokens on its first turn; this
  session's 91,144 is the number to beat and is dominated by material the orchestrator never needed at start.
- **Per-row growth** (5.7): one full row (plan, graph, review, close) must grow the orchestrator's context by
  under 60,000 tokens. Here 17 turns of reading grew it by ~228,000 (91k → 319k) with no row done: the
  reading pattern, not the work, is the cost.
- **Fix rounds**: none recorded in this repository (`--repo .`: no task files); the pilot's figure (44% of
  fix rounds after GREEN, 24 rounds blocked on a false sentence vs 14 on a code defect) stands until a 0.6
  project reports its own.

Sub-agent transcripts are reported, never gated (5.4): their context is the dispatcher's choice of prompt
plus their own reads.
